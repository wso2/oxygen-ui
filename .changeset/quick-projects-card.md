Add `ProjectCard`, a card for listing projects with an icon badge, title, description and a relative last updated time (`updatedTime`, with an optional `updatedTooltip`). The whole card can be made clickable with `onClick` or `href`, an optional delete button (`onDelete` / `actions`) appears on hover, long text is truncated with a tooltip, and `selected` highlights the card.
